# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Maintainers operating Ralph Loop from the terminal and Copilot side panels. They need to understand automation state quickly, approve guarded transitions, and intervene safely when work stalls.

## Product Purpose

Ralph Loop coordinates issue-driven coding agents through deterministic gates, isolated worktrees, and auditable run state. The dashboard and pipeline surfaces make that automation legible and controllable without weakening safety rules.

## Brand Personality

Operational, trustworthy, and direct. The interface should feel calm under failure, precise about consequences, and fast for experienced operators.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, ambiguous icon-only actions, hidden automation gates, success-shaped fallbacks, and dense status displays that force operators to infer whether an action is safe.

## Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious.
- Explain blocked actions before the operator clicks.
- Preserve deterministic gates in every interface.
- Keep evidence close to status and action.
- Prefer compact operational clarity over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Never rely on color alone for state. Respect reduced-motion preferences and provide visible focus, loading, success, disabled, and error states for every action.
